Open Science

Preprints, open data, open access research, and infrastructure that keep knowledge free.

arXiv

Open-access preprints in physics, mathematics, computer science, quantitative biology, and more.

PubMed Central

Free full-text archive of biomedical and life sciences journal literature.

Zenodo

CERN-backed repository for research data, software, and publications with DOIs.

Open Science Framework

Project management, open data sharing, and preregistration platform.

DOAJ

Directory of Open Access Journals – high-quality peer-reviewed OA journals.

Internet Archive

Books, journals, software, web history (Wayback Machine), and more.

Unpaywall

Finds legal open-access versions of paywalled research papers.

Semantic Scholar

AI-powered academic search engine for discovering scientific papers faster.

CORE

World’s largest collection of open access research papers.

bioRxiv

Free preprint server for biology. Read the latest research before formal publication.

DOAB

Directory of Open Access Books – thousands of free academic books.

Wikimedia Commons

Free media files under open licenses used by Wikipedia and researchers worldwide.

Free AI Platforms

The best free and freemium AI tools available right now for chat, research, images, and coding.

ChatGPT

OpenAI’s flagship AI. Strong free tier for conversation, writing, coding help, and image generation.

Google Gemini

One of the most generous free tiers. Excellent for long documents, research, and Google ecosystem users.

Claude

Excellent for writing, analysis, and long documents. Strong reasoning and large context window.

Perplexity

Best free AI for research with citations. Gives answers with sources.

Microsoft Copilot

Free AI assistant integrated with Bing and Microsoft tools. Good for search and everyday tasks.

DeepSeek

Powerful free models with very generous limits. Strong at coding and reasoning.

Grok

xAI’s AI with real-time knowledge from X. Useful for current events and direct answers.

Meta AI

Completely free AI from Meta. Available in WhatsApp, Instagram, and the web.

Mistral Le Chat

European open-weight models with a solid free tier. Good privacy-focused option.

HuggingChat

Free chat interface powered by open-source models from Hugging Face.

NotebookLM

Google’s free AI that works only with the documents you upload. Excellent for studying and research.

Consensus

AI search engine that finds answers from scientific papers.

Ideogram

One of the best free AI image generators for readable text inside images.

Leonardo.ai

Strong free daily credits for high-quality AI image generation.

Adobe Firefly

Commercially safer AI image generation with a free credit allowance.

GitHub Copilot Free

Free AI coding assistant with monthly completions and chat allowance for individual developers.

Cursor

AI-first code editor with a usable free Hobby plan.

Codeium

Free AI code completion and chat with generous limits for individuals.
