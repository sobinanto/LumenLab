Open Science

Preprints, open data, open access research, and infrastructure that keep knowledge free.

arXiv

Open-access preprints in physics, mathematics, computer science, quantitative biology, and more. The backbone of modern open research.

PubMed Central

Free full-text archive of biomedical and life sciences journal literature. Essential for open medical research.

Zenodo

CERN-backed repository for research data, software, and publications. DOI for everything. Perfect for open science reproducibility.

Open Science Framework (OSF)

Project management + open data + preregistration. Helps researchers share workflows and materials transparently.

DOAJ

Directory of Open Access Journals. High-quality, peer-reviewed open access journals across disciplines.

Internet Archive

Books, journals, software, web history (Wayback Machine), and more. A digital library for the world.

Unpaywall

Finds legal open-access versions of paywalled papers. Browser extension + open data.

Wikimedia Commons

Free media files (images, video, audio) under open licenses. Used by Wikipedia and researchers worldwide.

Semantic Scholar

AI-powered academic search engine that helps you discover scientific papers faster and more intelligently.

CORE

World’s largest collection of open access research papers. Excellent for finding free full-text articles.

bioRxiv

Free preprint server for biology. Share and read the latest research before formal publication.

DOAB

Directory of Open Access Books — thousands of high-quality academic books free to read and download.

Education

High-quality free learning materials, courses, textbooks, and interactive simulations.

MIT OpenCourseWare

Almost the entire MIT curriculum — lecture notes, videos, assignments — free for anyone in the world.

Khan Academy

Free world-class education in math, science, computing, economics, and more. Personalized practice.

OpenStax

Peer-reviewed, openly licensed college textbooks. Free digital, low-cost print.

PhET Interactive Simulations

Free science and math simulations from the University of Colorado. Research-based and beautiful.

LibreTexts

Open textbooks and educational resources across STEM and humanities. Continuously updated.

Project Gutenberg

Over 70,000 free eBooks — classic literature and public-domain works. Pure knowledge, no DRM.

Scratch

MIT’s free creative coding platform for kids and beginners. Learn programming by making stories, games, and animations.

Coursera (Audit)

Many university courses can be audited for free. World-class instructors, structured learning paths.

edX

University courses from Harvard, MIT, and others. Many can be audited completely free.

OpenLearn

Free courses and materials from The Open University. Excellent for self-paced learning.

GeoGebra

Free interactive math, geometry, algebra, and calculus tools used by millions of students and teachers.

Desmos

Beautiful free graphing calculator and classroom activities for learning mathematics.

OER Commons

Large library of free Open Educational Resources for teachers and self-learners.

Creative Tools

Browser-based and free creative software that lets anyone design, draw, code, and make without expensive licenses.

Photopea

Full-featured Photoshop alternative that runs entirely in the browser. Supports PSD files. Free with optional ads.

Excalidraw

Virtual whiteboard for sketching diagrams, wireframes, and ideas. Hand-drawn feel, collaborative, open source.

p5.js Web Editor

Creative coding environment based on Processing. Make interactive art, visualizations, and generative design in the browser.

CodePen

Front-end playground for HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Share demos, learn from others, prototype UI ideas.

Figma (Free)

Collaborative interface design tool. Free personal plan is extremely capable for UI/UX and prototyping.

Tinkercad

Free browser-based 3D design and electronics simulation. Perfect for beginners and education.

Radio Garden

Spin the globe and listen to live radio stations from anywhere on Earth. Beautiful, simple, inspiring.

Canva (Free)

Easy graphic design for social posts, presentations, posters. Generous free tier with templates.

Penpot

Open-source design and prototyping tool — a free alternative to Figma that runs in the browser.

diagrams.net

Powerful free diagramming tool (formerly draw.io). Great for flowcharts, mind maps, and architecture diagrams.

Coolors

Fast and beautiful color palette generator. Perfect for designers and creators.

Shadertoy

Create and explore amazing real-time shaders and generative visual art in the browser.

Twine

Free tool for creating interactive, nonlinear stories and text-based games. No coding required.
